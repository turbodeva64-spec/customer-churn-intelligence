Python Modular Pipeline (src/)

  • 1. src/config.py: Pydantic BaseModel settings for SQLite/PostgreSQL paths, random seed (42), 80/20 split, 5-fold CV, decision threshold (0.32), and visual palette.
  • 2. src/database.py & src/inspect_data.py: Dual SQLite/Postgres manager ingesting 7,043 rows into customer_churn_raw and flagging 11 whitespace strings in TotalCharges where tenure == 0.
  • 3. src/clean_data.py: Coerces blank TotalCharges to 0.0, encodes binary flags, and engineers tenure_group, customer_segment, and addon_services_count (0–6).
  • 4. src/eda.py: Computes segment KPIs and renders Visuals 01–10 at 300 DPI.
  • 5. src/train_model.py & src/evaluate_model.py: Fits leak-free ColumnTransformer (4 numeric + 43 one-hot features) with Logistic Regression & Random Forest; generates Visuals 11–13 and threshold tables.
  • 6. src/predict_customers.py & src/export_powerbi.py: Scores all 7,043 customers (Expected_Monthly_Revenue_Loss = MonthlyCharges * Churn_Probability) and exports the 5-table Power BI Star Schema.
